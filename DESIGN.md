# FirstMotion: Connected growth

## Direction
A full identity and layout redesign: an open, light, modern marketing studio with a clear technical capability. The only retained brand constraint is the FirstMotion name. A custom continuous ribbon expresses the connection between audience, AI and the next customer. No domino imagery, dark video intro, scroll-jacking, stock photography or invented client proof.

## Tokens
- Cobalt `#164FFF`: primary action and brand motion
- Navy `#15264A`: typography
- Deep navy `#101E3B`: AI workflow section
- Cloud `#F7F9FD`: hero and supporting surfaces
- Pale blue `#EAF0FB`: business-fit section
- Apricot `#FF8358`: a small functional AI/human handoff accent
- Body text `#50607A` on white and cloud; muted dark-section text `#BBC9E0`

## Typography and layout
Self-hosted Assistant 400/600/700/800, with a new large, bold sans-serif display treatment. Desktop uses an asymmetric two-column hero, a service disclosure list, a wide connected workflow, a four-step process, and editorial founder profiles. Mobile stacks every major section with a compact navigation menu and vertical workflow.

## Content architecture
1. Marketing, creative, websites and AI automation in a single clear hero
2. Familiar platform names, explicitly representing channels rather than clients
3. Four practical service areas with descriptive expandable details
4. Dedicated AI/automation section with a clearly labeled illustrative workflow
5. Four-step delivery process
6. Lead, ecommerce and B2B fit
7. Verified founder backgrounds
8. FAQs and a truthful WhatsApp draft handoff

## Interaction and accessibility
No third-party animation dependency is loaded by the new homepage. Native details, a lightweight mobile menu, existing bilingual engine, accessibility controls and fit-check modal remain. The site does not depend on motion or JS to reveal content. Keyboard focus, reduced motion, self-hosted fonts and RTL/LTR layouts are supported. A background socket restriction prevented local browser execution; consult the PR checks and review notes for actual visual verification status.

## Preserved operations
The existing WhatsApp number, email, Facebook destination, Vortex script and event names, language persistence, legal URLs, and legacy `#chain` / `#gap` links are preserved. No live CRM, AI backend or new tracking system was activated. Fit-check answers stay in the browser until the visitor explicitly continues to a prefilled WhatsApp message; WhatsApp still requires the visitor to review/send.

## Mobile detail refinement
Founder profiles use the same SVG stroke language as services, AI workflow and hero labels. Each founder has a compact identity row, full-width biography and three concrete areas of responsibility. Cobalt and muted apricot distinguish disciplines without relying on emoji glyphs or claimed client proof. The sticky header is fully opaque so scrolled text cannot show through it.
